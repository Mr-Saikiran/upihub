## UPI Hub 0.6.0

Install `techswitch-upihub-0.6.0.apk` on Android 7.0 or newer. It installs over
earlier 0.x builds signed with the same key.

### Pick the app first, then scan in it
Payment apps treat payments handed over by another app as coming from an
unknown source and often decline them (small shops, RuPay credit cards), while
the same QR scanned inside the app goes through. The home screen is now a
picker: tap Kiwi, Paytm, PhonePe or any of your UPI apps, it opens, and you tap
Scan there, so the payment is the app's own scan. Long-press a tile to make it
your default or hide it. Apps do not let other apps open their scanner screen
directly, so the app opens on its home screen, one tap from Scan.

**Scan here instead** keeps the earlier flow (scan in UPI Hub, then hand over).
It now sends the QR image to apps that take one, never modifies signed merchant
QRs, and offers another route when an app declines.

### iPhone (unsigned preview)
The same picker is the home screen; tiles open each app through its URL scheme.

Verified with unit and Robolectric tests; not yet tried by the authors with
every payment app.
