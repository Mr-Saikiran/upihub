## UPI Hub 0.5.0

Install `techswitch-upihub-0.5.0.apk` on Android 7.0 or newer. It installs over
earlier 0.x builds signed with the same key.

### Fewer declined payments
- **QR Share and Pay:** apps that take a shared image (Google Pay and PhonePe,
  and any other app that declares one) now get the merchant's QR as an image and
  read it themselves, as when you scan in the app. Payment links handed over by
  another app are what payment apps most often decline for small shops and
  RuPay credit cards. *Settings → Send the QR image* turns this off.
- **Signed merchant QRs are never modified.** Adding an amount to a signed QR
  breaks its signature, which payment apps must decline as tampered; the amount
  is now entered in the payment app for those.
- **When an app declines a link,** UPI Hub explains why and offers to send the
  QR image instead, or to save it to Photos and open the app to scan it there.

### iPhone (unsigned preview)
Signed QRs are no longer modified, and the payment sheet can share the QR image
to any app through the iOS share sheet.

Verified with unit and Robolectric tests; the QR-image route has not yet been
tried by the authors against every payment app.
