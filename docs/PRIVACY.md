# UPI Hub — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [date of publishing]_

UPI Hub (`techswitch.upihub`) is a QR scanner that hands UPI payment requests
to a UPI app you choose. This policy explains what the app does with data.

## Summary

UPI Hub **does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal
data**. The app has no internet permission, no account, no analytics and no
advertising. Everything happens on your phone.

## What the app uses, and why

| Data | Use | Leaves your phone? |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | Live preview to read QR codes. Frames are processed in memory and never saved. | No |
| Images you pick or share | Read once to find a QR code, then discarded. | No |
| QR code / payment details (UPI ID, payee name, amount, note) | Shown to you, then passed to the UPI app **you select** so it can complete the payment. | Only to that app, when you tap pay |
| List of installed UPI apps | Show you which apps can pay. | No |
| Settings (default app, app order, hidden apps) | Stored privately on your phone. | No |

UPI Hub never sees your UPI PIN, bank account or card details. The UPI app
you choose handles the payment under its own privacy policy.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — to scan QR codes. You can deny it and still pay from a
  screenshot or by typing a UPI ID.

## Children

UPI Hub is not directed at children under 18.

## Deleting your data

All data stays on your phone. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it
deletes everything.
